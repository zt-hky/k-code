## v0.1.0

First public release of K-code, a fork of [T3 Code](https://github.com/pingdotgg/t3code).

- New brand, name and installers: desktop apps for Linux, Windows and macOS, and a headless server/CLI.
- One-line installers at `kcode.agentbox.io.vn/install.sh` and `install.ps1`.
- **Oh My Pi** as a first-class provider with sign-in, thinking levels including `auto`, skills and plan mode.
- **Agent Hub**: live grid of running subagents (no pane cap, responsive columns, Live/All filter, maximize), a project-wide running-agents page, and per-agent Stop where supported.
- Batch subagent tasks split into one entry per task.
- **Full transcript mode** on web, desktop and mobile, with a toggle in Settings, the chat header, the command palette and `mod+alt+shift+e`.
- **Resume native sessions** with real titles, across projects, and renames written back to Oh My Pi sessions.
- Settings sync across devices connected to one server.
- Docs and product site at [kcode.agentbox.io.vn](https://kcode.agentbox.io.vn).
